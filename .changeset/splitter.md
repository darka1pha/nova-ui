New experimental `Splitter` (`@darkalpha/nova-ui/experimental`): resizable panes from a `panes` list (`id`, `label`, `content`, sizes, `collapsible`), built on `ResizablePanelGroup`, with a grip and a named handle between each pair. Takes the group's `direction`, `autoSaveId`, `onLayout` and `labels`.

Motion system and 25 new components. Themes gain a motion personality (subtle, standard, expressive) that drives new interaction tokens (hover lift, press scale, spring easing, motion-scaled durations, intent glows, focus glow, blur). Existing components gain hover/press/focus animations, a gliding Tabs indicator, drawn checkmarks and a toast countdown. New: Calendar, DatePicker, PinInput, TagInput, FileDropzone, Rating, Chip, CopyButton, CircularProgress, Stepper, Timeline, Stat, Sparkline, Banner, Carousel, Collapsible, HoverCard, ScrollArea, AspectRatio, Reveal, AnimatedNumber, Marquee, GradientText, SpotlightCard, AnimatedBorder. Also adds `pnpm install:local` for registry-free installs.
